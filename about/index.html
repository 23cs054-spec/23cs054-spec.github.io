Engineering Profile

Disciplined Engineering & Foundations

A background rooted in computer science fundamentals, modular software architecture, and international perspective.

What I Build

I build reliable web applications, distributed backend services, and structured system architectures. Whether architecting high-concurrency message queues in Node.js, designing normalized PostgreSQL relational schemas, or developing commercial frontend interfaces with sub-second paint times, my focus centers on resilience, clarity, and predictable execution. I prioritize native web primitives over bloated abstractions, ensuring software remains performant across constrained networks and high traffic loads.

How I Work

My development workflow begins before code is written. I map out system boundaries, data contracts, and UML class diagrams to validate assumptions early. In code, I enforce strict typing, separation of concerns, and comprehensive automated testing. I maintain disciplined Git hygiene with atomic commits, clear pull request documentation, and continuous integration checks. Writing code that is immediately understandable to teammates and future maintainers is as vital to me as passing unit tests.

What I'm Learning Next & Aspirations

I am deepening my expertise in distributed consensus protocols, event-driven microservices orchestration, and Linux container internals. Originally from Nepal and currently completing my degree in Coimbatore, India, I bring an adaptable, cross-cultural perspective to engineering teams. Looking forward, I am open to international engineering roles and remote teams that demand technical rigor and problem-solving velocity.
