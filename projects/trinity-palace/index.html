Case Study

Trinity Palace Website

Commercial marketing platform for a luxury resort, boutique hotel, and wedding venue in Bokaro, India.

Role
Lead Frontend Engineer & Designer
Timeline
2024
Tech Stack
HTML5, Modular CSS, Vanilla JS
Deliverable
Production Marketing Site

The Problem

Trinity Palace operates as an upscale resort and wedding destination in Bokaro, requiring a digital presence that mirrored the physical grandeur of its banquet halls and luxury suites. The existing web presence lacked brand distinction, loaded uncompressed photography slowly over regional 4G networks, and failed to communicate premium hospitality.

The objective was to deliver a high-converting, visually arresting web experience that conveyed understated elegance, loaded in under 1.5 seconds, and rendered fluid 60fps scroll animations across both desktop displays and budget smartphones.

My Role

I spearheaded the full frontend architecture and visual execution from concept to deployment. My responsibilities included:

  • Establishing an 80:20 modern minimal luxury color system: 80% crisp white/neutral negative space balanced by 20% refined warm metallic gold accents.
  • Building a zero-dependency animation engine utilizing native IntersectionObserver rather than heavy third-party scroll libraries (saving ~180KB of runtime JS).
  • Implementing modern image optimization with responsive picture elements, WebP compression, and content-visibility primitives.
  • Configuring local SEO schema (LocalBusiness / LodgingBusiness) to capture high-intent wedding venue booking queries in Bokaro.

Key Architectural Decisions

1. Vanilla JavaScript vs. Heavy Frameworks

A marketing showcase requires fast first-contentful paint (FCP), not complex client-side state reconciliation. By writing modular ES6 modules with no framework overhead, the total JavaScript bundle remained under 12KB gzipped, resulting in instant client load.

2. Hardware-Accelerated Scroll Choreography

Rather than binding to window scroll events which trigger forced layout recalculations and jank, scroll triggers were driven entirely via IntersectionObserver callbacks. All animations animate exclusively transform: translate3d(...) and opacity to remain strictly on the GPU compositor thread.

3. Responsive Asset Pipeline

Resort photography was processed into multiple responsive breakpoints (480w, 800w, 1200w, 1920w) in modern WebP format. Explicit aspect-ratio containers were specified in CSS to eliminate Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS = 0.00).

Challenges & Engineering Solutions

Challenge: Mobile Jitter on High-DPI Displays

Initial test builds revealed micro-stutter during rapid scrolling on mobile Safari due to heavy backdrop-filter blurs over large un-composited background images.

Solution: Isolated blurred header navigation into a separate render layer with will-change: transform and scoped the backdrop-filter to execute only after scroll displacement exceeded 30px, restoring stable 60fps rendering.

Challenge: Preserving Luxury Aesthetics in Light and Low-Contrast Modes

Gold metallic accents frequently fail WCAG AA text contrast thresholds (4.5:1) when placed directly on white backgrounds.

Solution: Restricted primary metallic gold exclusively to borders, icons, and non-text decorative badges, pairing all readable typography with a deep charcoal tone (#1e293b) to achieve a contrast ratio exceeding 9.8:1.
